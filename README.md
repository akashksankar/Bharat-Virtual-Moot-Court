## 🌐 What is CourtVerse?

**CourtVerse** is an immersive, real-time **virtual courtroom practice platform designed for law students and legal aspirants**.

It transforms traditional moot-court practice into an interactive digital courtroom where students can take different legal roles, interact with mentors, examine witnesses, present evidence, raise objections, and participate in realistic simulated hearings.

Instead of simply watching a video call, participants actually **enter a virtual courtroom**.

> 🧑‍⚖️ Judge  
> ⚖️ Plaintiff Counsel  
> ⚖️ Defence Counsel  
> 👤 Witness  
> 🧑 Victim / Complainant  
> 👮 Police Officer  
> 🧪 Forensic Expert  
> 👥 Spectators  
> 🤖 AI Participants

   │
   ▼
AI checks:
• Case facts
• Timeline
• Previous answers
• Evidence
• Contradictions

The AI should maintain case-specific context and consistency instead of generating unrelated answers.
